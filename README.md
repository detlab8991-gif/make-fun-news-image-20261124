One image summing up the world's news of **Tuesday 24 November 2026**,
built for the make.fun `news-image-20261124` bounty. It is laid out like a
newspaper front page: a navy masthead with the date, then seven illustrated
panels — one per story — each with a short headline, plus a footer strip
with the Indonesia detail.

## The seven stories

1. **Korea** — Russian military aircraft enter South Korea's air defence
   zone; Seoul scrambles fighter jets and briefly warns Ulleung Island.
2. **Nepal** — A magnitude 5.6 earthquake strikes Taplejung, felt across
   India, Bhutan, Bangladesh and China; no casualties reported.
3. **Space** — China names the Shenzhou-24 crew, including Pakistan's
   Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign astronaut bound for Tiangong.
4. **UAE** — A bus–truck collision in Ras Al Khaimah kills 11 people.
5. **Fiji** — All schools close to students 30 November–4 December as an
   El Niño drought deepens.
6. **Colombia** — A car bomb destroys the Meta governor's office in
   Villavicencio, injuring at least 12; the president blames traffickers.
7. **Indonesia** — The first supermoon of 2026 peaks at 21:53 WIB, with
   BMKG warning of tidal flooding on several coasts.

Every story above happened, or was first reported, on 24 November 2026.

## Files

- `entry.jpg` — the image, 1024×1024, generated in the build session
  and resized from the 1600×1600 render to exactly 1024×1024.
- `main.py` — copies `entry.jpg` byte-for-byte to `/output/entry.png`.
  No network, no other input, deterministic: two runs write identical bytes.
- `LICENSE` — MIT.
- `README.md` — this file.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
# wrote /output/entry.png (231257 bytes)
```
