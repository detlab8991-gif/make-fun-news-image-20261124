# News Image — Tuesday 24 November 2026

This entry is a single 1024 by 1024 image that sums up the world's
news of Tuesday 24 November 2026 for the make.fun bounty
news-image-20261124. It is laid out like a newspaper front page:
seven illustrated panels, each telling one story from that day,
plus a footer strip carrying the day's last headline.

## The seven stories

1. **Korea** — Russian military aircraft entered South Korea's air
   defence identification zone, and Seoul scrambled fighter jets in
   response.
2. **Nepal** — A magnitude 5.6 earthquake struck Taplejung. It was
   felt across the region, and no casualties were reported.
3. **Space** — China named the Shenzhou-24 crew, including
   Pakistan's Muhammad Zeeshan Ali, the first foreign astronaut to
   visit the Tiangong space station.
4. **United Arab Emirates** — A bus collided with a truck in Ras Al
   Khaimah, killing 11 people.
5. **Fiji** — Schools will close from 30 November to 4 December as
   the El Niño drought deepens.
6. **Colombia** — A car bomb destroyed the Meta governor's office in
   Villavicencio.
7. **Indonesia** — The first supermoon of 2026 peaked at 21:53 WIB,
   and BMKG warned of tidal flooding.

## Files

- `entry.jpg` — the finished front-page image, 1024 by 1024.
- `main.py` — a small program that copies `entry.jpg` to
  `/output/entry.png` so the image can be produced on demand.
- `LICENSE` — the MIT licence for this entry.
- `README.md` — this description.

## How to run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Running the program writes `/output/entry.png`. The output is a
byte-for-byte copy of `entry.jpg`, so repeated runs always produce
the same file, with no randomness and no outside input.
